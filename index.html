PRIVACY POLICY — MATTER 1B APP

Last updated: 8/28/2026

This policy describes what information the Matter 1B app ("the App") currently collects and how it's used, as of this version. The App is an early-stage, evolving project — this policy will be updated as functionality changes, and any material changes will be reflected here.

Third-Party Services

This App currently relies on third-party services, whose privacy policies change regularly. We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. We rely on the built-in security measures of the third-party services, which are not impermeable, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of any information submitted to the App.

Be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries where our third-party service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your own.

How We Use What We Collect

We save information solely to communicate with you about Matter 1B, evaluate submissions, and share relevant updates unless you request its removal. As the App evolves, retention practices may become more structured, and this policy will be updated accordingly. We do not sell this information to third parties. Submissions are accessible only to those administering the project.

No Warranty

The App is provided on an evolving, early-stage basis. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability, and we are not responsible for data loss related to third-party services we rely on.

What We Collect

  • Anchor Registration: When you register for an Anchor Badge, we collect your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Build Submissions: When you submit credentials via the Build tab, that information — along with your name, email, and phone number — is collected and stored.
  • Donations: The Fund tab uses an embedded donation form. Payment information you enter is collected and processed directly by the third party, not by us. We do not receive or store your card details. The third partys’ own privacy policy governs how they handle that data.
  • Analytics: We do not currently use any analytics or tracking tools ourselves. No cookies, pixels, or tracking scripts are added by us beyond what the third parties may inherently include as part of their own services.

Your Choices

You can request that your submitted information (name, email, phone, and any submitted links) be removed by contacting us at m1bi@mailfence.com.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently done so, we will take steps to delete it.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy can be directed to M1Bi@mailfence.com.
