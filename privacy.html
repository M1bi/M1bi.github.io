PRIVACY POLICY — MATTER 1B APP

Last updated: 10/24/2026

This policy describes what information the Matter 1B app ("the App") currently collects and how it's used, as of this version. The App is an early-stage, evolving project — this policy will change as the platform develops. If you register for an Anchor Badge and tick the box, we will email you updates about Matter 1B. You can unsubscribe at any time by following the unsubscribe link in any email or by emailing M1Bi@mailfence.com. We do not send marketing text messages or make marketing phone calls.

Who We Are

The App is run by Matter 1B. You can reach us at M1Bi@mailfence.com.

What We Collect

  • Anchor Registration: When you register for an Anchor Badge, we collect your name, email address, and, if you choose to give it, your phone number.
  • Build Submissions: When you submit credentials via the Build tab, that information — along with your name, email, and, if you choose to give it, your phone number — is collected and stored.
  • Donations: The Fund tab uses an embedded donation form. Payment information you enter is collected and processed directly by the third party, not by us. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Saved on your device: After you register, the App saves your name and a "registered" note in your own browser, so it can show your badge when you come back. This stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.
  • Analytics: We do not currently use any analytics or tracking tools ourselves. No cookies, pixels, or tracking scripts are added by us beyond what the third parties may add through their embedded services.

How We Use What We Collect

We use your information only to communicate with you about Matter 1B, review Build submissions, and send updates if you agreed to receive them. We use it because you gave it to us and agreed to this when you submitted the form.

We never sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone except the services listed below, which help us run the App.

Third-Party Services

The App relies on these services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google stores the information you submit through our forms.
  • Stripe processes donations made through the Fund tab.
  • GitHub hosts this website.
  • Tailwind provides the page's design files, which your browser loads when you visit.
  • Brevo is the email service we use to send updates to people who agreed to receive them.

Your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries where these services operate, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence. We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

How Long We Keep It

We keep your information until you ask us to delete it, or for up to 2 years after our last contact with you, whichever comes first.

Your Choices and Rights

You can ask to see, correct, or delete the information you submitted (name, email, phone, and any submitted links) by emailing M1Bi@mailfence.com. You can unsubscribe from update emails at any time using the link in any email.

If you live in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to object to how we use your information, to ask for a copy of it, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

No Warranty

The App is provided on an evolving, early-stage basis. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability, and we are not responsible for data loss related to third-party services we rely on.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently done so, we will delete such information promptly.

Changes to This Policy

The App is early-stage and this policy will change as it develops. We will update the date at the top when it does, and tell you about significant changes.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy can be directed to M1Bi@mailfence.com.
