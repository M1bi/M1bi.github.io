PRIVACY POLICY — MATTER 1B APP

Last updated: 8/28/2026

This policy describes what information the Matter 1B app ("the App") currently collects and how it's used, as of this version. The App is an early-stage, evolving project — this policy will change as the platform develops. By submitting your information through our forms, you explicitly consent to receive transactional and promotional communications from us, including emails, SMS text messages, and phone calls, at the contact details provided. Message and data rates may apply, and you can withdraw your consent or opt out at any time by emailing M1Bi@mailfence.com, following the unsubscribe link in marketing emails, or replying "STOP" to any text message.

Third-Party Services

This App currently relies on third-party services, whose privacy policies change regularly. We take reasonable steps to protect the information you provide, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Be aware that your information may be transferred to and processed in other countries where our third-party service providers operate, which may have different data protection laws than your country of residence.

How We Use What We Collect

We save information solely to communicate with you about Matter 1B, evaluate submissions, and share relevant updates unless you request its removal. As the App evolves, retention practices may change, and we will notify you of significant changes to this policy.

No Warranty

The App is provided on an evolving, early-stage basis. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability, and we are not responsible for data loss related to third-party services we rely on.

What We Collect

  • Anchor Registration: When you register for an Anchor Badge, we collect your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Build Submissions: When you submit credentials via the Build tab, that information — along with your name, email, and phone number — is collected and stored.
  • Donations: The Fund tab uses an embedded donation form. Payment information you enter is collected and processed directly by the third party, not by us. We do not receive or store your payment details.
  • Analytics: We do not currently use any analytics or tracking tools ourselves. No cookies, pixels, or tracking scripts are added by us beyond what the third parties may add through their embedded services.

Your Choices

You can request that your submitted information (name, email, phone, and any submitted links) be removed by contacting us at M1Bi@mailfence.com.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have inadvertently done so, we will delete such information promptly.

CONTACT

Questions about this policy can be directed to M1Bi@mailfence.com.
